Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Because our products are digital and access may be provided immediately after purchase, cancellations and refunds are handled carefully.

Before purchase

Please review the product description and included items before purchasing. Contact us before purchase if you have questions.

Access or file problems

If a purchased file is corrupted, incomplete, inaccessible or materially different from the product description, contact us promptly. We may first attempt to restore access or provide a corrected/replacement file.

Refund requests

Where a refund is legally required or otherwise approved, it will be handled through the payment provider and method used for the transaction, subject to applicable rules.

Misuse or abuse

Bad-faith claims, payment disputes after successful delivery without a genuine issue, or deliberate misuse may be investigated. Nothing here limits rights that cannot legally be excluded.
