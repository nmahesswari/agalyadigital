Refund Policy

Because Agalya Digital products are delivered electronically, please review this policy before completing your purchase.

1. Digital-product nature

The Freelancer Business Operating Kit — Advanced is a digital product. No physical item is shipped. Once digital access or delivery has been provided, the nature of the product may limit the circumstances in which a refund can be issued.

2. Before purchasing

Please review the Product, FAQ, Terms & Conditions, Delivery Policy, and License pages before purchasing. The product description explains what is included so you can make an informed decision.

3. Duplicate or incorrect payment

If you believe you were charged more than once for the same purchase, or a payment was processed incorrectly, contact us at support.agalyadigital@gmail.com with the transaction details so we can investigate.

4. Payment completed but product not received

If your payment was successful but you did not receive the product or delivery instructions, contact support as soon as possible. We will first attempt to resolve the delivery issue or provide the applicable access.

5. Technical problems

If a delivered file is corrupted, inaccessible, or materially different from the product description because of a technical issue, contact us with details of the problem. We may provide a replacement file, corrected access, or another appropriate resolution.

6. Change-of-mind requests

Because this is a digital product and delivery can provide access to downloadable resources, refunds for change of mind are not automatically guaranteed after delivery. Each request will be reviewed according to the circumstances and applicable law.

7. How to request help

Email support.agalyadigital@gmail.com and include your name, purchase email, transaction/order reference if available, and a short description of the issue. Do not send complete card numbers, passwords, or other sensitive payment credentials.

8. Review of requests

Refund or resolution requests are reviewed individually. Where a refund is approved, the method and timing of the refund may depend on the payment provider and the original payment method.

9. Fraudulent or unauthorized transactions

If you believe a transaction was unauthorized, contact your payment provider promptly and also notify us so that we can investigate the relevant order information.

10. Contact

For refund or payment-related support, contact support.agalyadigital@gmail.com.

Important: This policy is general business information and should be reviewed for compliance with the consumer-protection and e-commerce laws applicable to your business.
