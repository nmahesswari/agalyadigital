Delivery Policy

This policy explains how the Freelancer Business Operating Kit — Advanced is delivered after a successful purchase.

1. Digital delivery only

The Freelancer Business Operating Kit — Advanced is a digital product. No physical product, parcel, or printed material will be shipped.

2. What happens after payment?

After a successful payment through the available checkout, the purchase is processed for digital delivery. You will receive the applicable product access or delivery instructions electronically.

3. Delivery timing

Digital delivery is intended to be provided after successful payment and order processing. In some cases, delivery may be delayed because of payment verification, technical issues, email-service problems, or other circumstances outside our immediate control.

4. If you do not receive your product

If your payment was successful but you cannot find the product or delivery instructions, first check the email address used during checkout, including spam, promotions, and other filtered folders. If you still cannot find the delivery information, contact support.agalyadigital@gmail.com.

5. Information needed for support

When contacting support about delivery, please provide your name, purchase email address, transaction/order reference if available, and a short description of the problem. Do not send your full card number, banking password, OTP, or other sensitive payment credentials.

6. Incorrect contact information

Please provide accurate information during checkout where required. If delivery is affected by incorrect or inaccessible customer information, contact support so we can review the order and determine the appropriate next step.

7. Technical issues

If a download link or delivered file does not work, contact support. We may provide corrected access, a replacement file, or other appropriate assistance where reasonably possible.

8. Payment status

A payment attempt that is pending, failed, cancelled, or reversed does not by itself confirm successful product delivery. Delivery is associated with a successfully processed purchase.

9. Delivery and refunds

Delivery issues are handled first by attempting to restore or provide the purchased digital product. Refund requests are governed by the Refund Policy.

10. Contact

For delivery assistance, contact support.agalyadigital@gmail.com.

Important: Delivery methods and timing may be updated as the business's payment and digital-delivery systems change. The version published on this page is the current Delivery Policy.
