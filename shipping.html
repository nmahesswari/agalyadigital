Shipping & Digital Delivery

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Digital products only: Agalya Digital does not currently ship physical products.

Digital delivery

After successful payment, the purchased digital product is delivered electronically through the checkout/delivery system used for the transaction.

Download issues

If you cannot access a purchased file or it is incomplete or corrupted, contact us promptly. We will make reasonable efforts to restore access or provide a replacement where appropriate.

Physical shipping

There is no courier, postal delivery or physical shipping charge for current digital products.
