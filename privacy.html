Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Agalya Digital may collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website or contact us.

1. Information we may receive

Depending on how you use the website, we may receive information such as your name, email address, contact details, order or payment-related information, and messages you send to our support team.

2. Payment information

Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers such as Razorpay. Agalya Digital does not need to receive or store your full card, UPI, or banking credentials. Payment information may be handled by the payment provider according to its own privacy practices.

3. How we use information

We may use information to process and support purchases, deliver digital products, respond to customer questions, resolve payment or delivery issues, maintain website security, and improve our products and services.

4. Email communication

If you contact us at support.agalyadigital@gmail.com, we may retain the information in your message so that we can respond and maintain a record of customer-support requests.

5. Cookies and technical information

The website or services used with it may use cookies or similar technologies and may collect basic technical information such as browser type, device information, pages visited, or approximate usage data. These technologies may help the website function, understand usage, or improve the service.

6. Third-party services

Our website may link to or use third-party services, including payment providers. Information handled by those services is subject to their own terms and privacy policies. We recommend reviewing the relevant third-party policies when you use their services.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell customer information as a business practice. Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to process payments, deliver products, provide technical services, maintain security, or comply with legal obligations.

8. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through the website and support process. However, no online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for customer support, transaction records, legal obligations, dispute resolution, security, or legitimate business purposes.

10. Your choices

You may contact us to ask about information associated with your customer-support interaction or purchase, subject to applicable law and reasonable verification requirements.

11. Children's privacy

Our products and services are intended for general customers and are not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for the purpose of selling products.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact support.agalyadigital@gmail.com.

Note: This policy is general website privacy information and should be reviewed for compliance with the laws and payment-provider requirements applicable to your business.
